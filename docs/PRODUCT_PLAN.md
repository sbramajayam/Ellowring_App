# Ellowring — Phase 1 Product Planning (V1 Summary)

## Brand
- **Name:** Ellowring
- **Tagline:** Learn. Prepare. Build. Get Hired.
- **Visual:** Forest teal + sun accent; “career ring” motif

## Vision
From 11th Standard to First Job — Everything in One Platform.

## Mission
Build India's most trusted Education, Career & Hiring Ecosystem connecting Students, Colleges, Companies, Training Partners and Channel Partners through one AI-powered platform.

## Roles
Student · College · Company/HR · Training · Channel Partner · Admin

## V1 MVP scope (shipped in this codebase)
Public discovery site, auth (JWT/OTP), RBAC dashboards, career guidance + AI suggest heuristic, coaching, courses, college admissions listings, study abroad listings, internships, live projects, job portal + applications, wallet & coupons, notifications, admin overview.

## Revenue model (planned)
Course/coaching sales, hiring/HR subscriptions, partner commissions, premium AI tools (V2), white-label (V3).
